Audio BlendTwo sounds, two spectrogram layers. B sits on top of A. Blend them like images: what you see is what you hear.
SoundsTap A or B to record your voice or upload a file, or drop a file on them. The loop is as long as the longer sound, up to 30 seconds. The shorter one repeats to fill it.
Move BDrag sideways to shift B in time, up or down to shift its pitch. Double-tap resets.
TryThe same sound in A and B, B shifted 2 s, mode DIF: only what changed.
